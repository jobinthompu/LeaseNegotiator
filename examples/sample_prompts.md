# Sample Prompts for `lease_negotiator`

Paste any of the prompts below into `adk web` (`http://localhost:8000`) or `adk run lease_negotiator` to test the specialist agents end-to-end.

## 1. Live Monthly Lease Programs + Wave-1 Outreach (94550 / 100-Mile Radius)

```text
Compare this month's lease programs (captive lease cash, Tier-1 buy-rate Money Factor, and 36-month / 10,000-mile residuals) for all four models I'm shopping near ZIP 94550: Hyundai IONIQ 9, Kia EV9, Volvo EX90, and Volvo XC90 Plug-In Hybrid. Tell me which model/trim currently gives the sharpest effective monthly payment per dollar of MSRP, pick the best Wave-1 dealerships within 100 miles of 94550 to contact first, and draft copy-ready emails and SMS messages I can send today.
```

## 2. Audit a Padded Dealership Quote & Draft Counteroffers

```text
Please audit this dealer quote I got for ZIP 94550 (36 months, 10,000 miles/yr):
- Vehicle: Hyundai IONIQ 9 Performance Limited AWD
- MSRP: $72,900
- Dealer Selling Price before rebates: $72,400
- HMF EV Lease Cash: $13,000
- Dealer Protection Package (cilajet + wheel locks): $1,495
- Doc Fee: $85 | Acquisition Fee: $995 | DMV/Gov Fees: $820
- Residual: 57% | Money Factor: not disclosed on sheet
- Due at Signing: $4,999 (includes $1,800 cash cap reduction)
- Monthly Payment: $739/mo including 10.25% tax

Expose every markup or trap, calculate what my clean $0-cap-reduction counteroffer should be, and draft both a firm reply text to this salesperson and a competing price-beat email I can send to Central Valley Hyundai, Hyundai of Tracy, and Stevens Creek Hyundai.
```

## 3. Side-by-Side Competing Offers + Volvo MSDs vs. Zero-Down

```text

2. Dealer B (Sacramento): Volvo EX90 Plus Twin Motor ($79,485 MSRP) — $859/mo incl. tax with $2,400 due at signing (0 MSDs).

Compare them apples-to-apples on true effective monthly cost and % of MSRP. Then calculate how much putting down 10 refundable Volvo MSDs saves me over 36 months, and write a counter email leveraging corporate Volvo A-Plan eligibility (asking the dealer to run both A-Plan vs. straight 9% off MSRP + rebates).
```
